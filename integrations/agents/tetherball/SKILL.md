# Tetherball

Use `tether-review` for document and comment handling.

Maintain a shared account that the human and agent can inspect and rethink together. Choose its structure for its purpose, and reshape it when that purpose changes. Carry forward what still matters from the body and discussion.

Use questions and feedback to refine explanations, test assumptions and discover what to investigate next. Let findings revise the questions as well as the answers. The human’s steering helps maintain perspective and intent; it need not signal a failure or a need for more rules.

Threads can improve the shared account, open a separate line of work, or simply answer a question. Incorporate lasting understanding where it belongs; not every exchange needs a body addition. Treat exploratory examples and possibilities as such until they become decisions.

Throughout the session, revise toward clarity and precision. Continually replace superseded explanations, combine repetition and remove conversational scaffolding. Add detail where it improves understanding; preserve necessary rationale and uncertainty. Treat reading effort as a cost, not brevity as a quota.

Assume the human knows the workflow; omit usage tutorials and reminders.
